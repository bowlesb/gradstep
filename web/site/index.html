¶ Why I’m building Gradstep

On-device AI, cloud AI, and your own understanding.

I want AI to expand what we can understand. I’m starting with learning about AI itself: a field where following the reasoning matters as much as keeping up with the latest papers.

Gradstep brings different kinds of help into the same reading experience: local models to help prepare PDFs, cloud models to explain difficult ideas, and your curiosity to guide the conversation. The aim is to work through a paper until you can explain it in your own words.

¶ A reader built for understanding

Explanations, right where you’re reading.

A passage, figure or equation can be the starting point for a conversation. Explore the part you find difficult, check the source, and return to the paper with a clearer picture.

See what the figure is showing.

Ask about a figure or one of its parts. The assistant can place cropped details beside its explanation, so you can compare the interpretation with the image you’re studying.

Work through the equation.

Open an equation, adjust its size, and explore its symbols. Explanations distinguish definitions found in the paper from general mathematical background, with links to source paragraphs when available.

Test your understanding.

Study mode asks you questions and responds to your answers using the paper. A study map helps you keep track of the concepts you’ve covered and the ones you want to revisit. Give the idea a go in your own words.

A library that grows with your interests.

Give the papers you discover a place alongside the questions and notes they inspire.

  • Save papers and organize them with labels. Keep notes on the ideas you want to return to.
  • Discover related work, browse a feed of new papers, and ask for recommendations for your next read.
  • Your library, notes and conversations can sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac and web. PDF files and prepared reading copies stay on the device where you saved them.

¶ How it works

Local reading. Cloud help when you ask.

Start with arXiv HTML

Our strongest support is for arXiv’s official HTML. Gradstep prepares it on your device, bringing the paper’s sections, equations and figures into a layout suited to your screen.

Bring your PDFs

Open and keep PDFs on your device. The Apple apps also offer experimental on-device models that prepare a structured reading from a PDF. Results can be imperfect; the original remains available to check.

Choose when to ask AI

When you ask for cloud AI help, your question and relevant paper text, images or conversation context go to your selected model after consent. The original PDF file is not uploaded for conversion.

Encryption for your notes and conversations.

They are encrypted on your device before storage or sync, with keys held by your devices. Your library list and tags are handled separately by the service. Stored-history encryption does not hide the context you send for an answer from the AI provider. Read the privacy details.

¶ Where we are

Built by reading. Improved with you.

Gradstep is in private beta on iPhone, iPad and Mac through TestFlight, and in the browser. We’re improving it by reading real papers, checking the explanations and fixing what gets in the way of understanding.

If you’re learning about AI or working through scientific papers, bring your questions and tell us where the reading gets difficult. Help shape a reader that supports the way you think.
