¶ Why Gradstep

Agents can read for you. Understanding is still yours to build.

Research agents can now search a field, read dozens of papers and hand back a tidy report. That’s useful for knowing what exists. But a summary gives you a paper’s conclusions, not its reasons, and it’s easy to mistake one for the other.

Gradstep is built for the part you can’t delegate: working through a paper until you could explain it yourself. The AI explains. You stay with the source, and the source stays checkable.

¶ What it does

What Gradstep does

Figures, taken apart.

Ask about a figure and the assistant looks at the image itself. Its explanation comes in parts, each with its crop of the figure placed right where the answer discusses it.

Equations, one symbol at a time.

Tap an equation to open it large and set its size. Every symbol is explained beneath it, from the paper’s own definition with the paragraph it comes from, or marked as general meaning where the paper doesn’t say.

A tutor that asks back.

Study mode turns the roles around. It asks you questions, checks your answers against the paper, and keeps a map of what you know, what you’re learning and what to look at next.

Also in the beta

  • Listen to a paper as a short episode in two voices, one explaining and one pushing back.
  • Diagrams the assistant draws when a method is easier to see than to read.
  • A library and a feed of new papers, the same on every device you sign in on.

¶ How it works

What happens where.

The paper itself

Gradstep reads the official HTML version that arXiv publishes for a paper and prepares it on your device. Equations stay real math, the figures are the authors’ own, and the contents follow the paper’s sections.

PDFs stay with you

A PDF you add is opened and kept on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers. A model that reads difficult PDFs on the device itself is in testing.

Answers come from the cloud

When you ask a question, it goes with the paper’s text to Anthropic’s Claude models, which write the answer. Nothing is sent until you agree the first time. Privacy

¶ Where we are

Early, and changing every week.

Gradstep is in a private beta on iPhone, iPad and Mac through TestFlight, with a version in the browser. It focuses on machine-learning papers from arXiv, and it gets better by reading real papers with it and fixing whatever gets in the way.

If you read ML papers and want a say in what it becomes, we’d like to hear from you.
