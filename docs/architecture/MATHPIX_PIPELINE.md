# Mathpix processing and durable reader artifacts

> **2026-10-05:** The implemented, deployed flow is described in [PAPER_PIPELINE.md](PAPER_PIPELINE.md) (migration 014, Edge `paper-worker`). It replaces the quote/consent/`provider_attempts` design below with credit holds sized to a Mathpix `page_ranges` cap, a single submission grant per funded attempt, and explicit exits for every hold. The rest of this document is retained design background.

Status, 2026-10-04: the transport and bounded archive-container adapters exist. The Edge-compatible renderer [`timestep-mathpix-mathml-v1`](READER_RENDERER.md) renders the retained real Mathpix capture of one CC BY 4.0 paper (arXiv 2305.18290) into a validated `reader-bundle-v1` with MathML equations and JPEG figures, and native Core admits it. That bundle replaces the earlier synthetic `timestep-mathpix-mmd-v1` pilot fixture, which was removed together with its renderer and native profile. The Simulator journey has not been rerun with the new fixture. No paid submission, deployed acquisition worker, quote/consent route, durable hosted result collector or reader-artifact publication is enabled. One real capture does not establish general OCR accuracy or compatibility. The final reader requires this pipeline; original PDF delivery alone does not complete it.

## Separate acquisition from paid processing

Migration 010 defines account import intent, immutable exact-version metadata and asset approvals, membership fences and library change cursors. Installed migration 011 defines original reservations, verification and authorized-read contracts, with acquisition gates disabled; the local metadata execution work does not admit original transfers or paid OCR. See [Acquisition](ACQUISITION.md) and [Executor lifetime](EXECUTOR_LIFETIME.md) for the outstanding runtime and receipt boundaries. A subsequent additive contract must introduce the complete paid OCR lifecycle. Keep paid work disabled until that lifecycle and its failure schedules pass.

Reuse follows exact document bytes, provider/app scope and a versioned canonical OCR profile. An article title, DOI or arXiv identity alone is insufficient. Account library membership and private encrypted conversations remain separate from reusable publication assets. A renderer upgrade consumes retained raw OCR output without purchasing OCR again.

## Versioned provider profile

`_shared/mathpix.ts` implements fixed-origin `v3/pdf` transport. `science-reader-mmd-v1` freezes the complete options JSON and hashes a versioned domain, API, profile ID and those options. It retains equation tags, fonts and spacing; uses explicit inline/display math delimiters; preserves section numbering, hyperlinks, page breaks and margin notes; requests `mmd.zip`; and sets `metadata.improve_mathpix` to false. MMD and line data are collected separately. Renderer/sanitizer versions are outside this OCR identity.

The adapter captures an owned copy of the original PDF and submission checkpoint before suspension, checks the recorded byte and options hashes, and uses an opaque attempt UUID as its upload filename. Requests have a 30-second transport deadline, reject redirects and do not expose provider bodies or credentials in errors. Declared and actual response sizes are bounded; truncated uncompressed bodies, aborted reads and unexpected response formats fail. Limits are 64 KiB for status JSON, 8 MiB for MMD, 32 MiB for the archive and 16 MiB for line data. These are transport limits, not proof that parsing fits an Edge invocation.

No public route calls this adapter. Its `submitOnce` method does not itself authorize spending or guarantee durable at-most-once submission. A worker must first persist a fenced attempt and obtain the one-time submission grant described below. Any ambiguous POST outcome becomes `submission_uncertain`; the adapter never retries a paid POST automatically.

References: [PDF request](https://docs.mathpix.com/reference/post-v3-pdf), [shared options and statuses](https://docs.mathpix.com/reference/shared-types), [supported outputs](https://docs.mathpix.com/reference/supported-formats).

## Quote, consent and attempt identity

Retain `ocr_runs(document, provider, options_digest)` as logical work identity. A processing coordinator owns separate paid `provider_attempts`; current `job_attempts` are lease claims and must not be repurposed as financial attempts. Legacy job funding fields cannot be overwritten to model a retry. Gate legacy transitions for the new protocol.

A quote binds the verified original object, document hash and page count, OCR profile/app scope, provider/privacy policy, immutable rate version, customer-credit ceiling, service-spend ceiling, expiry and current membership/import intent. The authorization command has an immutable request digest and explicit consent version.

Under the documented lock order, authorization first reconciles exact command replay, then rechecks intent and scope. An existing compatible artifact is reused without an OCR hold. Existing active work accepts another account interest without changing its funder. New work atomically reserves customer credits, service spending and output capacity, creates one funded attempt and enqueues it. Waiters never become funders implicitly. Cancellation and reconciliation retain reserved command-receipt capacity even when ordinary additions are at quota.

## Submission, uncertainty and cancellation

Persist an attempt and submission intent before POST. `begin_submission(attempt, generation, request_digest)` grants permission only on its first transition. A replay reports current state and must not grant another POST, including when the first permission response was lost.

Timeouts, expired submit leases and lost responses retain both financial holds and leave ordinary resubmit queues. A narrow idempotent evidence route can record a late provider ID against that same attempt and request digest after its worker lease expires; this does not authorize stale publication. A uniquely verified match in the provider's searchable results can reconcile an accepted upload using filename, arguments, timing and provider ID. No result or several candidates does not prove nonacceptance. No automatic safe `v3/pdf` POST idempotency guarantee is assumed. [Mathpix result lookup](https://docs.mathpix.com/reference/pdf-results)

Before submission intent, funding cancellation fences the worker, settles zero, releases holds and leaves any remaining shared interests awaiting a fresh consenting funder. After submission intent, cancellation/removal detaches the account interest while collection and expense reconciliation continue. The submission gate checks current account membership/import intent under the account lock: a removal that wins prevents submission. A new paid retry requires a new authorization and attempt after the prior attempt has been reconciled and settled. Polling, collecting and rendering have separate retry counters and cannot initiate a paid retry.

## Collect, verify, render and publish

Wait for document status `completed`; 100% page progress is insufficient. Wait separately for the `mmd.zip` conversion's own completed status. Retain MMD, line/equation data and the original archive in controlled storage. Validate archive paths, duplicate names, expanded bytes, entry count, image formats/dimensions and every referenced asset before publication. ZIP signatures and basic output JSON validation in the transport adapter do not establish a usable bundle.

A raw-result content manifest identifies every required member by role, bytes and checksum; its durable publication record binds those members and the manifest to immutable stored-object IDs. Moving storage locations must not change the content identity. No temporary provider image URL may be required for durable reading. A pinned renderer emits sanitized offline HTML, stable block/equation/figure anchors, SVG math with LaTeX/assistive MathML, and bundled styles/fonts. Artifact publication validates the entire manifest, not just its JSON object. [Mathpix renderer source](https://github.com/Mathpix/mathpix-markdown-it)

`_shared/archive.ts` implements the `reader-zip-v1` container boundary: up to 32 MiB compressed and expanded, 16 MiB per file, 512 entries, and bounded ASCII relative paths. It rejects duplicate/case-colliding names, file/directory conflicts, symlinks, encryption, ZIP64, unsupported methods/extra fields, noncontiguous records and inconsistent local/directory/data-descriptor identities. It owns input before yielding and validates every actual expanded size and CRC before returning any result. This is a deliberately restricted profile, not general ZIP compatibility. Image contents, dimensions, MMD references and complete reader manifests still require separate validation. [ZIP specification](https://pkware.cachefly.net/webdocs/casestudies/APPNOTE.TXT)

Extraction uses `node:zlib`'s bounded output and engine-info APIs, with exact compressed-byte consumption checked. Local Node and Deno regressions verify truncated streams, trailing data, invalid stored-block lengths and dishonest expanded sizes. The first decoder failed the trailing/block probes, and local Deno's `DecompressionStream` accepted a truncated raw stream, so API availability alone is insufficient evidence. Keep these strict-rejection cases in the deployed-runtime admission check. Cancellation is cooperative between bounded entries; the hosted CPU/memory envelope and real Mathpix bundle compatibility remain unverified. [Node zlib API](https://nodejs.org/api/zlib.html)

Native integration must preserve generated stable heading IDs, resolve figures through verified bundle-scoped resources, and derive selected-equation source from the same parser nodes. Do not copy the archival blanket removal of pre-abstract figures, generic adjacent-div merging or ordinal regex equation association. Evaluate equation-heavy papers, tables, diagrams and offline transitions in the actual reader.

The independently reviewed local pilot is a generated, verified **Offline sample**. [`workers/reader-renderer`](../../workers/reader-renderer/README.md) pins Mathpix markdown-it and an independent HTML sanitizer/parser. The shared `reader-bundle-v1` manifest binds source hashes, renderer identity and each member's path, role, media type, bytes and SHA-256. `reader.json` carries heading/block/equation/figure identities. LaTeX, SVG and assistive MathML come from the same parser token; `source_form` distinguishes a complete equation environment from a delimited body. Fresh renderer processes prevent TeX macro state crossing documents. Source-controlled math styling, label ambiguity and incomplete resource sets fail publication.

The authored article contains numbered/aligned equations, a matrix, wide table, footnote and two original figures. It is neither a Mathpix capture nor a redistributed benchmark paper. Manifest `4851d8173451955cc36a455dc5d5fe0992a156d186c09f6fb291e05b4935f0b4` binds renderer build `5a7e0a6362962af49f559035b5a4d85cf8a9a2cd376cbabda648005098d8260f`, seven members and 149,019 member bytes. Twenty-three renderer tests pass, including repeat-build identity, cross-document macro isolation and nested-process timeout cleanup. Bounds and dependency notices are recorded in its README. This narrow profile accepts authored MMD and PNG only; it does not establish real OCR output compatibility or hosted execution limits.

The Swift caller pins the trusted manifest digest, owns every verified resource byte and exposes only exact manifest URLs through a nonpersistent WebKit scheme handler. Reader and figure sheet use that same snapshot. Page JavaScript is disabled; the app-owned bridge runs in an isolated content world. A selected equation crosses the bridge by bundle/block/equation ID and resolves its source from the verified sidecar. Its exact text is included in the existing encrypted draft/message transaction. Explicit equation taps expose the existing selection actions, with outside/Escape/native dismissal and pan suppression. ImageIO accepted a truncated PNG in regression testing, so native admission now independently checks the restricted PNG container, CRCs, bounded complete zlib extent and scanline filters before bounded ImageIO decoding. File hashes or a decoder's success flag alone are insufficient.

Paid OCR and real-account artifact delivery remain separate milestones. The [encrypted install index, complete library cache and selected-bundle reopening](NATIVE_OFFLINE_BUNDLES.md) are implemented in local format 6 / SQLite schema 5. The existing iPhone/iPad Simulator journeys install under a synthetic account/library binding, terminate, reopen real encrypted SQLite without a sample-loader fallback, use Contents/equations/figures and retain an encrypted paper question after another restart. That is storage/reader evidence, not Mathpix compatibility or production download evidence. Reuse this implementation rather than add another install index. The current reading location is transient, while selected source text is durably encrypted; structured selection anchors are not yet persisted through drafts. Native VoiceOver activation and actual-paper parity remain release gates alongside the documented offline acceptance checks.

Commit durable raw publication and corresponding settlement atomically once required outputs and usage evidence are verified. Rendering can then retry independently. Terminal provider/collection/render failure also needs a settlement path so a broken renderer cannot leave holds indefinitely. Provider deletion is a separately retried cleanup operation after verified durable collection; it does not remove our financial reconciliation obligations.

Initially retain all published originals, raw results and render manifests. Garbage collection covers expired unpublished reservations and orphan uploads only. At capacity, reject new work. Future eviction requires a reviewed availability/replica contract because immutable approvals and artifacts point to specific object identities.

## Local slice: raw outputs to the existing reader

Implement a local, deterministic adapter and fixture journey before another network worker. Its end is a Mathpix-shaped retained result converted into a fully verified bundle, installed through the existing native coordinator, reopened offline and used to save an equation question. A synthetic provider response establishes only the adapter contract. It must be labelled as such and cannot be reported as a successful Mathpix conversion. Saving the question uses the current encrypted message transaction; an AI answer is a separate provider/consent/settlement path.

### Raw capture and reopen contract

The pure module [`_shared/mathpix-result.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/mathpix-result.ts) uses the existing [`mathpix.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/mathpix.ts) types/profile identity and [`unpackReaderZip`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/archive.ts). It accepts bytes, not URLs, credentials, a fetch function or a submission callback. Its APIs are:

```ts
captureMathpixResult({ binding, status, conversion,
  standaloneMMD, archive, linesJSON }, signal?): Promise<MathpixRawResult>

openMathpixResult({ manifestBytes, expectedSHA256, rawMembers }, signal?):
  Promise<MathpixRenderInput>
```

`MathpixRawResult` contains exact canonical manifest bytes, their SHA-256 and three owned raw members. `MathpixRenderInput` contains the verified raw manifest/expected digest and the complete, owned expanded archive map. It contains no transport authority. Reopening checks the three retained raw members and repeats archive validation against the manifest before supplying render input. Share this validator rather than implement a second ZIP decoder in the renderer. The initial local bridge may hand these checked members to the existing bounded renderer process; TypeScript types alone do not make the receiving process trust them.

The closed `mathpix-raw-result-v1` manifest has these fields:

| Field | Binding |
|---|---|
| `format` | Literal `mathpix-raw-result-v1`. Canonical sorted-key UTF-8 JSON with one trailing LF; at most 256 KiB. No unknown fields. |
| `document` | Document UUID, verified original object UUID, exact SHA-256, byte length and verified page count, supplied from the existing immutable original/attempt checkpoint. The adapter checks structure and equality, not authorization. |
| `attempt` | Logical OCR-run UUID, funded attempt UUID, opaque provider-scope ID and provider job ID. No funding account, credentials or provider URLs. These remain server-side raw provenance. |
| `ocr` | `api: "v3/pdf"`, `profile: "science-reader-mmd-v1"` and the exact `mathpixProfile()` options digest. Renderer versions do not enter this identity. |
| `completion` | Bounded normalized `MathpixStatus` and `MathpixConversion` snapshots for that same provider ID, with completed states, no deletion or reported error. Retain nullable provider pipeline/page fields honestly; any supplied page counts must agree with the verified original. Counts are not invoice evidence. |
| `outputs` | Exactly `standalone.mmd`, `output.mmd.zip`, `lines.json`, each with fixed role, byte length and SHA-256. Preserve the raw bytes, including whitespace. |
| `archive_entries` | The complete sorted file inventory returned by the ZIP validator: exact path, expanded length and SHA-256. No more than 512 entries. |
| `render_source` | The one selected archive MMD entry's exact path, length and SHA-256, also present in `archive_entries`. |

Own all input buffers and bounded checkpoint/status scalars before the first hash/extraction await. Check status, conversion, profile and checkpoint consistency again on reopen; reject partial or substituted members without producing render input. Cancellation produces no partial result. Errors use a finite enum such as `binding_mismatch`, `output_incomplete`, `output_limit`, `archive_invalid`, `mmd_ambiguous`, `unsupported_content` and `cancelled`; no raw MMD, URLs or provider error bodies enter operational logs.

Keep the transport ceilings: 8 MiB standalone MMD, 32 MiB ZIP and 16 MiB line JSON, at most 56 MiB of raw member payload. Expanded ZIP bounds remain 32 MiB total / 16 MiB per file / 512 entries. This is a logical payload bound, **not** a host-memory reservation: owned inputs, expansion, hashing, parsed JSON, image decoding and renderer processes need additional headroom. A complete capture can be retained even when rendering later reports an unsupported construct. Raw capture does not confer permission to publish a reader or settle an invoice.

For this first profile, select exactly one case-sensitive `.mmd` file in the archive. Zero or several are unsupported; do not choose the first, largest or standalone response as a fallback. Preserve standalone MMD independently: its bytes, figure paths and formatting may differ from the archive's MMD. Neither byte equality nor identical equation/line ordinals is required. Validate line JSON's finite UTF-8/object/`pages` envelope against the retained page count, but retain its contents as **untrusted auxiliary data**. This slice does not use line geometry for reader anchors, equation association or billing; a typed geometry consumer needs its own reviewed validation before use.

Reuse [`ReaderBundleLoader.verify(manifest:expectedSHA256:members:)`](../../apps/apple/ScienceReader/Services/ReaderBundleLoader.swift) for native PNG/JPEG structural and ImageIO admission, then [`ReaderBundleCoordinator.install(binding:manifestData:members:)` and `open(_:)`](../../apps/apple/ScienceReader/Services/ReaderBundleCoordinator.swift) for atomic encrypted installation and scoped reopening. Core hash verification alone does not admit native images. Preserve exact reader manifest bytes, the existing 32 MiB/512-member reader bounds, one-install rule, local quotas, quarantine/outage semantics and account/session/access-revision fences. The offline fixture supplies an explicitly synthetic `ReaderArtifactBinding(scope, documentID, artifactID, manifestSHA256)` and real encrypted store; it cannot create real library membership. Production needs an authenticated artifact descriptor binding those same immutable identities to the current library item/availability revision, plus bounded authorized member delivery. The manifest digest must come from that trusted descriptor, never from the same arbitrary download being verified. Publish only after every member is durable and independently checked, then project the existing import/library records under current intent/removal fences. Neither a raw-capture result nor an installed local fixture authorizes that publication. Reuse existing Paper/Contents/figure/chat navigation; do not add a second PDF-only reader as this milestone's endpoint.

### Offline acceptance before provider or hosted enablement

1. **Separate sources and exact binding:** a small synthetic result deliberately gives standalone and archive MMD different bytes and image paths. Only the declared archive MMD is rendered; all three raw hashes remain exact. Reject wrong attempt/provider/profile/page binding, missing output and ambiguous MMD selection. Prove caller buffer/checkpoint mutations during an awaited hash cannot change the owned capture; later changes to retained bytes must fail reopening.
2. **Complete resources:** cover nested MMD paths, repeated/unused archive images, missing assets, case collisions, traversal/encoded paths and external image URLs. No fixture may trigger a fetch. Corrupt/truncated PNG and ZIP streams, invalid image dimensions and a rehashed malformed sidecar fail without a partial bundle. Substitute, omit or add an expanded member between raw reopening and the receiving renderer process; the renderer must refuse before parsing MMD. Tables, complete equation environments, aligned/numbered math and figure captions survive unchanged under the admitted profile.
3. **Independent retry and determinism:** reopen retained raw members in a fresh process, render twice with the same pinned runtime/build and compare complete artifacts. Force one rendering failure and retry successfully without changing the raw identity or calling any provider submission method. A mock transport can assert a POST count of zero for this path; that is not a test of real paid-submission idempotency.
4. **Backward compatibility and native rejection:** load the unchanged authored fixture through both directory and memory APIs. Accept the new exact profile pair and provenance; reject crossed/unknown pairs and contradictory source/image provenance. Run the shared malformed-image checks for the new profile, including complete-container/truncated-deflate and trailing-deflate cases.
5. **Actual offline reader/question journey:** install the rendered DPO bundle, terminate/relaunch with no network or authored-sample fallback, use Contents, text/equation selection and figures, save a paper question, return to its passage and restart again. Verify the saved question once and the same immutable artifact/draft context. Existing sign-out, quarantine, commit/retirement and capacity schedules continue to apply. Earlier passes used the removed synthetic bundle; rerun with the DPO fixture.

The raw adapter, renderer and explicit Swift compatibility check are implemented locally and require no new SQL or runtime activation. The retained DPO capture supplies the actual three Mathpix outputs under the exact OCR options; its archive uses `./images/*.jpg` crops referenced by `\includegraphics`, which the renderer admits. One paper does not establish crop/figure completeness or parity across papers.

## Accounting, privacy and operations

Provider page/progress fields do not establish an invoice amount. Record a pinned-rate expense estimate and later reconcile aggregate provider periods with immutable adjustments. One aggregate invoice cannot be forced into the existing unique job-specific invoice-reference shape. Customer credits remain separately bounded by the accepted quote. [Usage endpoint](https://docs.mathpix.com/reference/ocr-usage)

`improve_mathpix:false` does not establish zero retention for PDFs. Describe the exact account/endpoint retention policy, including provider-held page images/text and any verified account override, before obtaining consent. Do not claim encrypted processing: the provider receives the uploaded paper. Private cloud-chat disclosures are separate. [Retention](https://docs.mathpix.com/concepts/data-retention), [privacy options](https://docs.mathpix.com/concepts/privacy)

Acquire budget periods before capacity policy, funding account, document, work, attempt and object locks. Re-read identities after locking. Never acquire budget/account locks from a helper while already holding a work/attempt lock. Publication creates a durable projection outbox and releases work locks; fanout then updates one account under the library lock order. Each update checks current interest and preserves removal fences and established artifact pins.

Required deterministic schedules include simultaneous funders, lost authorization and submission-grant responses, accepted POST with lost ID, late ID after lease expiry, cancellation on both sides of submission, removal/re-add before publication, delayed uploads, quota exhaustion with cancellation available, missing bundle members, renderer failure without another POST, cleanup/publication races and interrupted fanout. A real equation-heavy import through offline native reading remains the release gate after these contracts pass.
