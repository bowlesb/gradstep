# Design audit 1 (2026-10-06)

Independent agent review of `.artifacts/visual/audit1` (23 scenarios × iPhone 17 Pro / iPad Pro 11″ × light/dark) against the owner's principles (see [DESIGN](../DESIGN.md)). Verbatim report:


**Coverage.** All 23 scenarios exist in all four folders, but three of them don't show what they were meant to:

- **`library-add`** shows the Sign-in sheet on both devices, because + asks a signed-out user to sign in first. I could not audit the Add-paper sheet, and `signin` is just a copy of this screen.
- **`library-detailed`** is identical to `library-table` on both devices.
- **iPad wide layout:** neither the Browse column nor the top segmented switch appears. The iPad Pro 11" is 834 pt wide in portrait, and `RootView` only switches to the wide layout at 850 pt or more. These need re-capturing in landscape.

## 1. Uniform pattern spec

- **Screen options:** each screen has one `…` menu using the `ellipsis` symbol, never `ellipsis.circle`. It is always the rightmost button in the top bar. Inside it, in this order:
  - View, Sort and Show as submenus that display the current choice.
  - The screen's own actions.
  - "Report this screen", last.
- **Top-bar positions:** the left side is for "where to go" (Contents, History). The right side is for creating something (+, compose), followed by `…`.
- **Top-bar buttons:** a 44 pt glass circle with a 17 pt semibold symbol in the accent colour.
  - On iOS 26 use `.glassEffect(.regular.interactive(), in: Circle())`; on iOS 17–25 use `.regularMaterial`.
  - The Library's custom header uses this same `HeaderButton`.
- **Margins:** 16 pt for all iPhone content and bars.
  - Floating bars (tabs, selection actions) sit 12 pt from the screen edge.
  - On iPad, every scrolling column is at most 720 pt wide and centred.
  - The reader's text column is the only exception.
  - Current values vary by screen: Library header 16, Library rows 18, chat Paper/General switch and message box 12, chat answer text 28, reader 23.
- **Vertical spacing:** use the steps 4/8/12/16/24. Lines within a row are 4 apart, rows have 12 pt padding, header to content is 8, and sections are 24 apart.
- **Text sizes:**
  - Primary: `.headline` for titles, `.body` for reading.
  - Secondary: `.subheadline` in `.secondary` for author lines and banners.
  - Tertiary: `.footnote` in `.secondary` for metadata and status.
  - `.caption` only for tab labels; never `.caption2` for content. The accent colour is only for things you can tap.
- **Banners:** one shared `StatusBanner` component.
  - Look: inset 16 pt, rounded corners (`RoundedRectangle(12)`), a light accent fill (8 %) for information or orange (12 %) for problems, a 17 pt symbol, and a `.subheadline` message.
  - Actions: a chevron on the right, or at most two semibold text buttons.
  - Placement: shown only when the user can act on it. It's the first item in the scrolling content, so it scrolls away, except in chat, where it sits above the message box. Normal states show nothing.
- **Sheets:** a centred title in the bar.
  - Sheets that only show information get "Done" on the right (`.confirmationAction`).
  - Sheets where the user is doing a task get "Cancel" on the left (`.cancellationAction`) and the confirm button on the right.
  - Heights: medium-or-large (`[.medium, .large]`) for short lists, medium (`.medium`) for sign-in.
- **List rows:**
  - Text: a `.headline` title (up to 2 lines), a `.subheadline` author line (1 line), optional `.footnote` detail (up to 2 lines), and `.footnote` status with a 13 pt symbol.
  - Layout: 16 pt side padding, 12 pt top and bottom, and at most one icon on the right.
  - Style: rows that open another screen show a chevron and normal text; rows that perform an action use accent-coloured text.
- **Empty states:** `ContentUnavailableView` with one sentence and one prominent button (`.borderedProminent`). Hide any control that can't work in that state.
- **Details on demand:** screen options go in `…`. Per-item actions go in the long-press menu, with swipe for delete-type actions. Long explanations go behind a row you tap into, not inline.

## 2. Findings

**F1 · Library table on iPad · margins (P1), hierarchy (P3) · high · styling**
- **What I see:** in `ipad-light/library-table.png` and the dark version, titles are cut off mid-word at the left edge ("ing machine", "ng Laws…"). The Title column has no header, and the Status column is cut off on the right ("Not downloac").
- **Why:** the app shows the table once the screen is at least 700 pt wide (`tableMinimumWidth = 700` in `Services/LibraryMetadata.swift`). The columns defined in `LibraryTableView.swift` lines 57–96 need about 1,150 pt at their preferred widths, and about 840 pt at minimum before spacing.
- **Change:**
  - Work out the threshold from the columns actually shown, or set it to 980, so iPad portrait falls back to Detailed rows.
  - Make the column headers grey (`.secondary`) and colour only the column currently used for sorting.

**F2 · Top-bar buttons differ between tabs · uniformity (P5) · high · styling + placement**
- **What I see:**
  - **Library:** + and … are 36 pt grey discs (`headerIcon()`, `RootView.swift` line 1045). They also sit 8 pt lower than the top bar on other tabs (`.padding(.top, 8)`, line 328), so the header jumps when you switch tabs.
  - **Paper:** 44 pt glass buttons, with `ellipsis.circle` drawing a ring inside the glass ring (`PaperView.swift` line 129).
  - **Chat:** has no `…` menu at all.
  - **Report:** Paper's menu has "Report this screen"; Library's doesn't.
- **Change:**
  - Turn `headerIcon()` into the shared HeaderButton, remove the 8 pt top padding, and make the search field 44 pt tall.
  - Switch Paper to `ellipsis`.
  - Give Chat a `…` on the right with text size, All diagrams and Report this screen.
  - Add "Report this screen" to Library's menu.

**F3 · Library "…" menu · options tucked away (P2), uniformity (P5) · high · placement**
- **What I see:** on iPhone (`library-options.png`) the menu has 12 rows and ends in a stray divider, with All chats, All diagrams and Refresh hidden below the fold. On iPad it scrolls, with All diagrams cut off and Refresh hidden.
  - The "Layout" and "Sort by" section headings don't appear (the inline pickers swallow them), but "Show" does.
  - Only the layout rows have icons.
  - In both this menu and Paper's, the icons are system blue instead of the app's indigo.
- **Change:**
  - Use three submenus (`.pickerStyle(.menu)`): "View as", "Sort by" and "Show".
  - Follow them with All chats, All diagrams and Refresh, then Report this screen, each with an icon.
  - If the icons are still blue, set the window's `tintColor` from AccentColor.

**F4 · Sign-in and status messages · uniformity (P5), tucked away (P2) · high · styling + placement**
- **What I see:** the same "you need to sign in / something went wrong" message gets five different treatments:
  - **Library (`library.png`):** a 52 pt full-width band that never scrolls away and also shows above search results.
  - **Chat (`chat-credits.png`):** plain caption text with two inline buttons that wrap onto a second line.
  - **Store (`store.png`):** "Sign in to buy credits." with no button, so it's a dead end.
  - **Feed:** a small button.
  - **Settings:** an accent-coloured row.
- **Change:**
  - **Library:** use `StatusBanner` as the first row of the list.
  - **Chat:** put a banner above the message box with "Get credits" and "Try again" buttons.
  - **Store:** use `ContentUnavailableView` with a Sign in button.
  - **Library refresh and pending-change messages** (`RootView.swift` lines 212–233): use the same banner.

**F5 · iPad column widths · margins (P1), open space (P4) · medium · styling**
- **What I see:**
  - **Chat (`ipad-light/chat-conversation.png`):** messages are limited to 720 pt (`ChatTranscript.swift` line 113), but the Paper/General switch, paper link and message box run the full width, so nothing lines up.
  - **Library:** abstract lines run about 800 pt wide.
  - **Settings:** the cards span the whole screen.
- **Change:**
  - **Chat:** apply the same 720 pt limit (`.frame(maxWidth: 720)`) to its header, error row and message box.
  - **Library list and Settings form:** add centred side margins with `.contentMargins(.horizontal, max(16, (w − 720)/2), for: .scrollContent)`.

**F6 · Library rows · hierarchy (P3), margins (P1) · medium · styling**
- **What I see:** in `library-metadata-list.png`:
  - Authors (12 pt) are smaller than the abstract (15 pt), which is the wrong way round.
  - The metadata line is 11 pt (`.caption2`), with 8 pt between every line.
  - On iPhone, the title of the open paper is accent-coloured and looks like a link.
- **Change:** in `PaperRow` and `ImportRow`:
  - Authors `.subheadline`; abstract and metadata `.footnote`.
  - 4 pt between lines; row padding from 18/14 to 16/12.
  - Colour the open paper's title only in the iPad split view.

**F7 · Chat messages · open space (P4), margins (P1) · medium · styling**
- **What I see:** in `chat-sources.png`:
  - Answers sit in grey bubbles (16 pt outside plus 12 pt inside), which squeezes equations.
  - In dark mode the bubble is almost invisible against black.
  - Messages are cut off sharply under the paper link; the top line is sliced in half.
- **Change** (`ChatTranscript.swift` line 108):
  - Drop the bubble background for assistant answers, and keep it for the user's messages.
  - Fade the top edge: `.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .top)` on iOS 26, otherwise a 12 pt fade.

**F8 · Chat header and message box · tucked away (P2), open space (P4) · medium · placement**
- **What I see:**
  - The top bar, the Paper/General switch and the paper-link row use about 150 pt before the first message.
  - "Saved on this device" appears under the message box in some states but not others.
  - The hide-keyboard button only appears while typing and makes the text field narrower (compare `chat-keyboard` with `chat-conversation`).
- **Change:**
  - Make the bar title two lines: the conversation name, then the paper title in small text that you tap to go back to the paper. That's still one tap.
  - Show save/sync status only when something is wrong.
  - Remove the hide-keyboard button and let scrolling dismiss the keyboard (`.scrollDismissesKeyboard(.interactively)`). Keep its ⌘. shortcut on a hidden button.
  - Use 16 pt margins for the message box and the switch.

**F9 · Sheet buttons and heights · uniformity (P5) · medium · styling + placement**
- **What I see:**
  - **Dismiss buttons:** Sign in has "Close" on the right, and Add paper has "Cancel" on the right (`RootView.swift` line 880).
  - **Store:** its "Done" is bold while other sheets use regular weight. Its "Restore" is a greyed-out button in the spot where Cancel normally goes.
  - **iPad wide layout:** the Settings "Done" is on the left.
  - **Heights:** Sign in, All chats and Store open at full height and are 60–70 % empty.
- **Change:**
  - Follow the spec: Cancel on the left for tasks, Done on the right otherwise.
  - Move Restore to the store's footer (`.storeButton(.visible, for: .restorePurchases)`), and make the Terms link accent-coloured.
  - Open Sign in at medium height, and the history sheets at medium-or-large.

**F10 · Tab bar · hierarchy (P3), uniformity (P5) · medium · styling**
- **What I see:** on every screen the Feed icon is smaller than Chat's, and its label sits about 3 pt higher than the others. The selected tab is shown only by colour. Icons are sized by font only (`RootView.swift` line 33).
- **Change:** give each icon a fixed 28 × 24 frame, use the filled symbol for the selected tab, and put a light accent capsule (12 %) behind it.
- **Keep the custom bar:** the system `TabView` moves the tabs to the top on iPadOS 18+, which would change navigation.

**F11 · Settings · tucked away (P2), hierarchy (P3), uniformity (P5) · medium · placement**
- **What I see:**
  - Similar rows look different: All chats and All diagrams are accent link text, Acknowledgements has a chevron, and My reports is black with an icon.
  - Only 3 of the 6 Testing rows have icons.
  - Two privacy paragraphs fill a whole screen.
  - Version and Environment are split between the Account and Testing sections.
- **Change:**
  - Make the rows follow the spec, with no icons.
  - Put the privacy text behind a "Privacy & data" row. The chat consent screen keeps its own disclosure.
  - Add a final "About" section with Version, Environment and Acknowledgements.
  - Add small and large text-size icons at the ends of the two text-size sliders.

**F12 · Sign-in sheet · uniformity (P5) · medium · styling**
- **What I see:** three different button shapes. Apple has 10 pt rounded corners, Google is an outlined box with 4 pt corners, and email is a 57 pt pill. In dark mode, a white Google button sits under a white Apple button.
- **Change:**
  - Make all three 50 pt tall with 12 pt corners. Apple: `NativeAuthenticationControls.swift` line 31. Google: `NativeGoogleAuthenticationButton`. Email: `.buttonBorderShape(.roundedRectangle(radius: 12))`.
  - Use Google's dark button in dark mode.
  - Open the sheet at medium height.

**F13 · Reader top bar · open space (P4), tucked away (P2) · low · styling + placement**
- **What I see:** "Offline sample" is a separate grey band under the bar (`PaperView.swift` line 60), which adds a hard edge in both themes. "Smaller text" and "Larger text" take two rows in the menu.
- **Change:** show "Offline sample" as a small second line under the title. Combine the two size options into one row of A− / A+ buttons (`ControlGroup` with `.compactMenu`).

**F14 · Selection bar on iPad · margins (P1) · low · styling**
- **What I see:** a full-width glass bar with four words in the middle.
- **Change:** remove `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` before the material (`PaperView.swift` line 192) so the bar fits its content.

**F15 · Feed · tucked away (P2), open space (P4) · low · placement**
- **What I see:** when signed out, the topic chips are still tappable even though nothing can load. They are grey text on grey and only 36 pt tall.
- **Change:** hide the chips when signed out or when loading failed, give them a 44 pt tap area, and use normal text colour for unselected chips.

**F16 · All chats and diagram sheets · hierarchy (P3), uniformity (P5) · low · styling**
- **What I see:** conversations are accent link text, while diagrams in the same list are normal navigation rows (`RootView.swift` lines 777–781). Diagrams use the diagram library's default lavender theme.
- **Change:** add a second line with paper and date to conversation rows, and take diagram colours from the app's accent colour.

## 3. Top 12 changes to make now
1. Raise the table's width threshold and fit the columns (F1).
2. One glass button style, `ellipsis` everywhere, Library header padding 0 (F2).
3. Library menu as submenus, with Report this screen (F3).
4. One `StatusBanner`, plus a Sign in button on the store (F4).
5. Consistent sheet buttons and heights; Restore moves to the footer (F9).
6. 720 pt columns on iPad (F5).
7. Library row text sizes, spacing and padding (F6).
8. Answers without bubbles, plus a soft top edge (F7).
9. Paper link in the chat title, status only on problems, no hide-keyboard button (F8).
10. Tab icon sizing and selected-tab capsule (F10).
11. Settings row layout, privacy behind a row, About section (F11).
12. Matching sign-in buttons (F12).

## 4. Keep
- **Detailed rows:** the app's best row layout; the spec is based on it.
- **Library search:** the one-row header, and Cancel plus Library/arXiv appearing only while searching.
- **Reader:** its typography, the iPad reading column and the equation numbers.
- **Selection bar:** text labels rather than icons.
- **Contents sheet:** opening at medium height.
- **Chat:** rendered equations and numbered sources.
- **Library rows:** the long-press menus.
- **Colour:** the light/dark accent colour pair and the Apple button inverting in dark mode.
- **Navigation:** small inline titles in the top bar throughout.
